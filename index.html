The Wright Brothers' 1908 contract in Akoma Ntoso and LegalRuleML

The Articles of Agreement of February 10, 1908, between the Signal Corps, United States Army, and Wilbur and Orville Wright, for the Army’s first airplane, with Signal Corps Specification No. 486 attached. The price was $25,000, on a sliding scale by the speed attained on trial. The text is marked up in Akoma Ntoso, and the rules are in LegalRuleML, embedded in the same file.

View the contract

How it looks

The original is a printed form filled in by typewriter. The stylesheet follows it, with the two kept apart in the markup. It uses modern fonts, so it isn’t a facsimile, and the signatures aren’t the real ones.

Why a contract

Contracts aren’t the usual use of Akoma Ntoso. This is an experiment in what fits, and the notes list what did and didn’t.

How it was made

The XML and the rules were generated by Claude for Grant Vergottini from page images of a later publication of the contract. Claude didn’t write a word of the contract. Part of Articles V and VI is missing from that copy and is marked. The rules are an interpretation, not part of the contract.

Files
